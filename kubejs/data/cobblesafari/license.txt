Changes, made for the Campfiremons modpack (from CobbleSafari 0.4.1):
- loot_table/entities/balloon.json, loot_table/underground_ominous_oasis.json: the Master Ball entry is removed.
- loot_table/blocks/buzzwole_plant.json, loot_table/blocks/pheromosa_plant.json: the block-state ranges are
  written as strings, so Minecraft 1.21.1 can load these tables (the originals fail to parse and drop nothing).
- wonder_trade/pokemon_groups/: the legendary, mythical, paradox and Ultra Beast species are removed from the
  type pools; the legendaries, mythical, paradox and ultrabeast pools (and their _vanilla twins) list other
  species instead.
- gts/unique_offers/hayley_mew.json, gts/unique_offers/hayley_phione.json: the offer asks for the same species it
  gives (a Mew or a Phione) instead of a Glaceon or a Leafeon.
The wonder_trade and gts files are written by tools/cobblesafari_trades.py.
