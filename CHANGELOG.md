# Changelog

Each `## vX.Y.Z` section becomes that version's GitHub Release notes (`tools/release/build.py`).

## v0.1.51

Already playing? Just press Play: the pack updates itself.

- **Ultra Space wormholes come at random times, and a bit less often.** After each wormhole, the next one opens somewhere
  between 30 and 90 minutes later (an hour on average), so they no longer bunch up (three opened within half an hour
  once). As before, the wait only counts down while someone who's reached Stage 6 is in the overworld, and the wormhole
  opens next to one of them.
- **One Medicinal Brew on Create.** Create: Cobblemon Integrations' Medicinal Brew mix (no heat) is gone, with the
  medicine mixes that needed it: Klinks 'n' Klangs' brew (in a heated mixer) makes all the same medicines, so nothing is
  lost. Brew you've already made with the old recipe can't be used any more; Medicinal Brew bottles still empty into the
  Klinks 'n' Klangs one.

## v0.1.50

Already playing? Just press Play: the pack updates itself.

- **Better Muffling:** a Muffling Block (4 wool, 4 leather and a note block) turns down sounds around it: pick the sound
  category, volume, range and particular sounds, and Private Mode makes it affect only you. Surround one with gold for the
  Advanced version.
- **Ultra Beasts come from Ultra Space wormholes.** No more wild Ultra Beasts anywhere (the End already went in v0.1.49;
  swamps, deserts, bamboo jungles, peaks and spooky biomes now too). Wormholes now open only next to players who've reached
  Stage 6, and much more often: about every 30 minutes while one of you is in the overworld (it was about every 11 hours,
  next to anyone). Right-click it to step in. CobbleSafari's Ultra Warp Ride still has its own.

## v0.1.49

Already playing? Just press Play: the pack updates itself.

- **No more phantom "max IV" Paradox spawns at the Kanto city gyms.** Six of the city gyms (Pewter, Viridian, Vermilion,
  Celadon, Fuchsia, Cinnabar) were built with Pokémon left inside them: perfect-IV Varoom, Ampharos, Electrode, Iron
  Valiant, Walking Wake and Archaludon, plus a Bulbasaur in each. Each new gym let them loose as wild Pokémon, and the
  Iron Valiant and Walking Wake were announced in chat like real spawns. They're gone from new gyms, and copies in gyms
  that already exist are removed when their area loads.
- **No more wild Ultra Beasts in the End.** They now come from Ultra Space and CobbleSafari's Ultra Warp Ride, plus the rare
  overworld spawns (swamps, bamboo jungles, deserts, peaks and spooky biomes). The End keeps its other Pokémon.
- **No more phantoms.** They don't spawn anywhere, whether or not you sleep. Phantom Membranes still drop from Noibat,
  Noivern and the Dreepy line.
- **Wear an Elytra as an accessory** (Elytra Slot): put it in a back slot in the Curios menu and keep your chestplate on.
  The back slot holds two, so a backpack and an Elytra fit together.

## v0.1.48

Already playing? Just press Play: the pack updates itself.

- **Quest book order fixed.** Stage 3 is back between Stage 2 and Stage 4; v0.1.47 had put it last in Gym Challenge.
- **Type icons in the gym chapters** show their own type when you hover over them, and link to its page. Since v0.1.44 they
  showed the previous stage's type (Stage 3's Grass icon described Electric), and Stage 0's had nothing.
- **Refined Storage works with EMI's + button:** it fills the Crafting Grid and Pattern Grid from your network (Refined
  Storage - EMI Integration).
- **Shiny parents can pass it on.** An egg is shiny 1 in 256 with one shiny parent and 1 in 8 with two (normally 1 in 8192).
  Parents with different original trainers are 4 times likelier again: 1 in 64 and 1 in 2. A shiny Ditto counts.
- **Catch streak milestones in chat:** at 10, 20 and 40 of a species in a row you're told the streak and the shiny odds it
  unlocked. The running streak still shows above your hotbar.

## v0.1.47

Already playing? Just press Play: the pack updates itself.

- **Shiny chains** (Cobblemon Unchained and Cobblemon Counter). Catch the same species in a row and it's more likely to turn
  up shiny for you in the wild, when fishing and at Poké Snacks: 1 in 4096 after 10 catches, 1 in 2048 after 20 and 1 in 1024
  after 40 (normally 1 in 8192). Catching any other species, or another form such as Alolan, starts the streak over; knocking
  Pokémon out doesn't. You get a chat message when a chain brings out a shiny, and only you can catch that one.
- **Counter**, a new item that lists your streaks: craft it from stone, a stone button and redstone, then hover over it. Your
  catch streak also shows above the hotbar from the second catch in a row.
- **Stage 3 in the quest book** now has "The Road to Erika" before the gym, like Brock's: the level cap, where to go, and that
  no key fights stand in the way. The other gyms already have their key fights in line.

## v0.1.38

Already playing? Just press Play: the pack updates itself.

- **Light level and chunk border overlays (More Overlays Updated).** Press F7 to show where mobs can spawn
  (blocks too dark to be safe) and F8 to show chunk borders. If those keys don't work for you, set "Toggle Light
  Overlay" and "Toggle Chunk Boundaries" in Options → Controls.
- **The Bumblezone.** A new dimension full of bees. Throw an Ender Pearl at a bee nest or beehive to get in, and go
  past the bottom or top to get out. It has its own Pokémon (Combee, Cutiefly, flower and bug Pokémon, Unown,
  Beldum, Spiritomb and more), structures to explore, bee armour and the Bee Queen's trades. Careful: take their
  honey and the bees come after you.
- **Transfer Labels.** Sneak-right-click a label onto a block to move items or fluids into or out of the block next
  to it, with filters. Made from a hopper, redstone and a chest (a bucket for fluid labels).
- **Ender Pearls from Pokémon.** Put a Pokémon that knows Teleport (Abra, Ralts, Natu, Elgyem…) in a
  pasture with an Ender Chest and a chest nearby. Every two or three minutes it fetches an Ender Pearl from the Ender
  Chest and puts it in the chest. Teleport has to be one of its four moves, and walkers like Natu and Ralts work more
  steadily than floaty Abra.
- **One kind of sprinkler.** The separate copper Sprinkler from v0.1.33 is gone: any you still have disappears when
  you update. Use the Create Slice & Dice sprinkler instead: pipe water into it to keep farmland wet, or liquid
  fertilizer (mix Tree Fertilizer or Organic Compost with water) to make crops under it grow.

## v0.1.36

Already playing? Just press Play: the pack updates itself.

- **Create schematics: Alt + scroll works.** Relics' Active Abilities key used Left Alt too, which took the mouse
  wheel away from Create's schematic tools. It now defaults to J. That only changes the default for new installs: if
  Alt + scroll doesn't switch schematic tools for you, open Options → Controls, search "Active Abilities" and set it
  to J (or press Reset).
- **Rare Pokémon are back to normal odds.** Rare, ultra-rare and Alpha spawns (Ditto, starters and their
  evolutions, Alpha herds and so on) appear as often as Cobblemon intends again: about three to six times more
  often than since v0.1.13.

## v0.1.35

Already playing? Just press Play: the pack updates itself.

- **Much better quest rewards.** Every quest now gives items from its own mod on top of its XP: Create parts for
  Create quests, Oritech materials for Oritech, storage upgrades for storage, Poké Balls, candies, potions and
  evolution stones for Pokémon quests, and so on, scaled to how big the quest is. Bigger quests also pay
  CobbleDollars (500, 2,000 or 5,000), and the gym and stage rewards are doubled.
- **Claim them again.** All rewards are new, so quests you've already finished now have their new rewards waiting.

- **CobbleSafari.** A Safari Zone, the Sinnoh Underground, Distortion World and Ultra Space adventures with bosses,
  hot-air balloons with loot, egg nests, secret bases and a Rotom Phone with the GTS and Wonder Trade. Legendaries
  still follow the stage locks: Wonder Trade doesn't hand them out, and the GTS's NPC trades for one ask for a
  Pokémon of the same kind.

Already playing? Just press Play: the pack updates itself.

## v0.1.30

Already playing? Just press Play: the pack updates itself.

- **Lapis stays in the enchanting table (Easy Magic).** Your lapis and item stay in the table when you close it, and
  a re-roll button gives you new enchantment offers for a little lapis and XP. Torches and grass no longer block
  bookshelves. Already have an enchanting table? Break it and place it again to switch this on.
- **Pick your enchantment (Enchanting Infuser).** A new table where you choose exactly which enchantment you want and
  pay XP for it, with no luck involved.
- **Friendlier anvils (Easy Anvils).** Items stay in the anvil when you close it, repairing and combining get much
  cheaper the more you do it, renaming is free (and supports colour codes), and you can fix a damaged anvil with an
  iron block.
- **A busier End (Moog's End Structures).** 25 new structures out on the End islands: spires, watchtowers, citadels,
  meteorites, End forests and huge crashed ships. Nobody had been to the End yet, so it has been reset to generate
  with them everywhere. Clair's gym and the Kanto League are still where they were.
- **More enchantment combos (Universal Enchants).** Enchantments work on more gear: Infinity on crossbows, Looting
  and Sharpness on tridents, Sharpness on axes and more. Infinity and Mending now go together, and so do Multishot
  and Piercing.
- **Bone meal on everything (Universal Bone Meal).** Grow sugar cane, cactus, vines, nether wart, coral and other
  plants vanilla bone meal ignores, by hand or from a dispenser.

## v0.1.29

Already playing? Just press Play: the pack updates itself.

- **Waystones are free.** Teleporting between waystones no longer costs experience levels.
- **Waystones are shared.** New waystones (ones you place, and ones in land nobody has explored yet) show up for
  everyone straight away, without each player visiting them first. Waystones you've already found stay as they are.

## v0.1.28

Already playing? Just press Play: the pack updates itself.

- **Better caves in new land (YUNG's Better Caves).** Land nobody has visited yet gets bigger, more varied caves:
  winding cave systems, underground lakes and rivers, flooded caverns, and some deep regions full of lava. Places
  you've already explored don't change, so a cave can stop where new land meets old.
- **Diesel and oil (Create: Diesel Generators).** Pump crude oil with a pumpjack, refine it into diesel and gasoline,
  and burn fuel in compact engines for lots of rotational power. Oil is under deserts, savannas, badlands, plains and
  oceans (use the Oil Scanner to find a good spot), and that works in land you've already explored too.
- **Oritech and Diesel Generators share oil.** Either mod's crude oil now works in either mod's refinery and in
  Oritech's Fuel Generator. Their diesel and biodiesel already worked in both.
- **Ultimine for berries and apricorns.** Hold the Ultimine key (` by default) and right-click a ripe berry bush or
  apricorn to pick the whole patch at once.
- **Handy commands (FTB Essentials).** `/sethome` and `/home` (up to 3 homes), `/tpa <name>` to ask to teleport to a
  friend, `/back` to return to where you died or last teleported from, `/spawn`, and `/rtp` for a random spot within
  2,500 blocks of spawn. Teleports take 3 seconds to charge. Also `/enderchest`, `/crafting`, `/trashcan`, `/hat`,
  `/nick` and a few more.
- **Create Utilities.** Void Motors send rotation to another Void Motor anywhere, Void Chests and Void Tanks share
  items and fluids across any distance, and the Gearcube splits rotation in every direction.
