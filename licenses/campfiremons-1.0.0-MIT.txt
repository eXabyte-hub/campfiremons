mods/campfiremons-1.0.0.jar is the Campfiremons pack's own mod (Diamond-Tipped Crushing Wheels for Create), built
from tools/mod/ in this repository with `./gradlew build`. Its code and its one texture
(textures/block/diamond_tipped_crushing_wheel_plates.png, drawn by tools/mod/gen_texture.py) are ours. Its models only
refer to Create's crushing wheel model by name; none of Create's files are copied into it.
